Refund Policy

When we refund subscription payments, how to cancel and how long a refund takes. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

This policy explains when Trovo Tech Solutions refunds a subscription payment for its software, including Baylior. Because every new business can use the software free during the trial before paying, payments are generally not refundable except in the cases below.

1. Free trial first

Every new business can start a free trial without paying anything. We encourage you to try the software fully during the trial before you buy a plan.

2. When we refund

  • First payment, within 7 days: if you are not satisfied, you can ask for a full refund of your first subscription payment within 7 days of paying it. Your account then becomes read-only.
  • Charged twice or wrongly: if you were charged more than once for the same invoice, or charged an amount different from your order, the extra amount is refunded in full.
  • Payment taken but no service: if your payment was received but your company could not be set up and we cannot fix it, the payment is refunded in full.

3. When we don't refund

  • Renewal payments, once the new period has started.
  • Unused time when you cancel during a period: the subscription stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and is then not renewed.
  • Users or add-ons removed during a period.

4. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by calling or emailing us. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you keep full access until the end of the paid period, then read-only access for 90 days to export your data, as described in our Terms.

5. How to ask for a refund

  1. Email info@trovotechsolutions.in or call +91 90426 72148 with your company code, the invoice number and the reason.
  2. We reply within 2 working days.
  3. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show it.
